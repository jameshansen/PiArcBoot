## What's changed

### New

- **Shaders.** The Game menu has a Shaders submenu. CRT-Royale-Pi draws the picture with the scanlines, phosphor mask and glow of a CRT monitor, and CRT-Pi is a lighter scanline and mask shader. A Raspberry Pi 400 keeps up with either, in a window or full screen. The choice holds for every game, and Off takes it away.
- **Games load much faster.** The floppy drive hands a game its data far quicker than a real drive, while the game itself runs at its usual speed. Fireball II reaches its menu after 20 seconds where it took 60, and Gods, James Pond and Magic Pockets finish loading in under half the time.
- **A3000 speed.** A game added on the My Games tab can run at the speed of an A3000's 8 MHz ARM2 rather than a 25 MHz ARM3, for a game made for the slower machine. Its Settings can also load its floppies at a real drive's speed.
- **Pause.** Pause in the Game menu, or Ctrl+Alt+P, stops the game until you choose it again.
- **Quick save and restore.** Ctrl+Alt+S saves to the slot selected for Auto Save and Ctrl+Alt+R restores it.
- **Keyboard in the launcher.** Ctrl+Tab switches tabs, Up and Down move through the games, and Return plays or installs the one selected.
- **My Games discs can be reordered or removed** one at a time in a game's Settings.
- **Installs can be cancelled**, from the progress box or by closing the window.

### Changed

- **Full screen at 60 frames a second on a Raspberry Pi 400**, where it drew about 40 before.
- **The window's size is the same for every game.** 1x, 2x and 3x Scale in the Game menu open every game at that size. While a shader is on, 1x Scale isn't available.
- **Classics lists the installed games first**, then the ones available, each under its source.
- Save asks before replacing a save in another slot.
- Each game opens in one window at a time, and starting it again while it's open says so.
- A window size you drag to is kept for the next time the game starts.
- The About tab links to the project, its issues and the Discord, and credits the shaders.

### Fixed

- **Pipe Mania** no longer stops with an address exception. The ROM is ROO311 v1.0.8.
- **Fire & Ice** starts without an address exception, and **Virtual Golf** waits in the desktop for its course.
- **A disc that boots by itself**, such as Starch added on My Games, stays behind the splash until it shows a screen of its own.
- **Star Fighter 3000's Settings box** no longer draws its disc table over its buttons.
- **Haunted House's disc request** can be seen, as a game shown cropped to its windows shows the whole screen while an error box is open.
- A save that can't be written says "Slot N couldn't be saved", for Save and Auto Save alike. Saves find their discs wherever the data folder has moved, and a save of another game's isn't restored.
- The Settings box always fits, growing the window or scrolling its disc table. Play says what is wrong with a game that can't start.
- Updating or removing PiArcBoot touches only its own files, so a portable copy's Data folder and anything else in the folder stay. A damaged installer says so and runs nothing.
- Add Game refuses empty or damaged disc images and discs another game already has, takes .Zip in any case, and says when discs are left out.
- A damaged JFD image can no longer take gigabytes of memory.
- `piarcboot --help` lists the commands and `--version` gives the version.
