## What's changed

### New

- **Seven more JASPP games:** E-Type II, Alone in the Dark, Confusion, Cyborg, Word Up Word Down, Xenon 2: Megablast and Manchester United.
- **Alone in the Dark** installs to a folder the machine reads directly, as its own installer would put it on a hard disc. Its copy protection asks for an item from its manual.
- **Xenon 2** runs from its application in a folder the machine reads directly, with its second disc in the drive for its copy protection.

### Fixed

- A game whose disc fails to boot keeps its window open, where v1.1.2 closed it a few seconds after the desktop came up.
