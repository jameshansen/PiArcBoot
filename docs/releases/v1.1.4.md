## What's changed

### New

- **No manual checks.** Alone in the Dark, Battle Chess and Empire Soccer 94 skip the questions they ask from their manuals, and Oh, No! More Lemmings starts without the original Lemmings disc. The changes to their code come from ADFFS by Jon Abbott for JASPP, which the About tab credits.
- **E-Type II starts on the desktop**, at 640 by 512, with its options on its icon bar menu: Game Type, Tracks, Keys and the players' cars. A click on its icon starts a race, and it comes back to the desktop after each one.
- A game's presets can change its code once it is in memory, with `patch=` lines, as `presets/README.md` describes.

### Fixed

- E-Type II's window stays open between races and closes once Quit is chosen from its icon bar menu, where v1.1.3 closed it after the first race.
- A game that waits on the desktop for the player, such as Virtual Golf, has its disc's folder closed once its icon is up.

ROM311 is ROO311 v1.0.10, whose HostLink says when a task stops.
